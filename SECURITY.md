Reporting a Vulnerability

We take the security of our engineering tools seriously. If you believe you have found a security vulnerability, please report it using the process below:

How to Report

Please do not open a public GitHub issue for security vulnerabilities. Instead, please report them privately:

Primary Contact: Reach out via Golden Kumar Micheal (LinkedIn).

Provide Details: Include a summary of the issue, steps to reproduce, and any potential impact.

Encrypted Communication: If you prefer to share technical details via a secure document, please mention this in your initial LinkedIn message.

What to Expect

Acknowledgment: You will receive an initial response within 48-72 hours.

Validation: We will investigate the report and confirm the vulnerability.

Resolution: Once confirmed, we will work on a fix and notify you when the update is ready.

Disclosure: We follow a coordinated disclosure model. We ask that you do not disclose the issue publicly until a fix is released.

Disclosure Policy

We are committed to fixing vulnerabilities as quickly as possible. We encourage responsible disclosure and will not take legal action against researchers who act in good faith and follow this policy.
